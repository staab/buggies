# Credits

The vehicles are drawn with these models. Their files are in `packages/client/public/models/`.

| Vehicles | Model | Author | Licence |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Race car, police car, fire truck, pickup, sports car, small car, ambulance, go-kart | [Car Kit](https://kenney.nl/assets/car-kit) (`race`, `police`, `firetruck`, `truck`, `sedan-sports`, `hatchback-sports`, `ambulance`, `kart-oobi`) | [Kenney](https://kenney.nl) | [CC0 1.0](https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/) |
| Tank | [Tank](https://poly.pizza/m/Dc4k4CooN3) | [Quaternius](https://quaternius.com) | [CC0 1.0](https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/) |
| Semi truck | [Cargo Truck](https://poly.pizza/m/Fy3WI3uXNQ) | J-Toastie | [CC BY 3.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/3.0/) |

The semi truck is J-Toastie's tractor unit; the trailer in the file is not drawn. The models are
otherwise used as published, apart from being scaled, and having their wheels taken off the body so
that the simulation can turn them.
